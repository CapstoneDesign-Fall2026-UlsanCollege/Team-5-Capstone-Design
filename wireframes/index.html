Why this order
  • Subject & Task Creation is the easiest first screen: one subject form, one task form, browser storage.
  • Study Plan is the value screen: pending vs completed, grouped by subject, sorted by deadline.
  • Quiz & Result closes the demo loop: 50 questions, marks and percentage.
  • No login, no cloud, no AI in the MVP.

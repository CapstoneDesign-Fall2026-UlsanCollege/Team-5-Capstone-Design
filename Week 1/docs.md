## Idea 1

Smart Study Companion

* **Project name:** Smart Study Companion
* **Target user:** College students
* **Problem:** Students find it difficult to organize their study tasks, remember assignment and exam deadlines, and prepare effectively for exams.
* **Smallest useful version:** Add subjects, study tasks, and deadlines; mark completed tasks and take simple multiple-choice quizzes.
* **Midterm demo could show:** Create a study plan, mark study tasks as completed, take a short quiz, and view the quiz score.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Keeping the study planning and quiz features simple and easy to use without making the application too complicated.

## Idea 2

pocket card

* **Project name:** PocketCart
* **Target user:** College students and families
* **Problem:** Students and families may lose track of their spending while shopping and find it difficult to manage grocery purchases within their monthly budget.
* **Smallest useful version:** Set a monthly budget, add income and expenses, add grocery items with prices, and mark purchased items.
* **Midterm demo could show:** Set a monthly budget, create a grocery list, add grocery prices, mark items as purchased, and view total spending and remaining budget.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Making expense tracking and grocery list management quick and simple enough for users to use regularly.

 Idea 2

* **Project name:** BudgetBasket
* **Target user:** College students and families
* **Problem:** People may forget what they need to buy and lose track of their spending while shopping, making it difficult to stay within their budget.
* **Smallest useful version:** Set a shopping budget, add grocery items with prices, and mark items as purchased.
* **Midterm demo could show:** Set a budget, create a grocery list, add item prices, mark purchased items, and display total spending and remaining budget.
* **Big risk / unknown:** Making grocery and expense tracking quick and simple enough for users to use regularly.

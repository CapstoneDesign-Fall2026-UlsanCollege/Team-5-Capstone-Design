# Midterm Demo Script

**Team:** Team 5 – Smart Study Companion  
**Week:** 7–8

## Opening

- **Project name:** Smart Study Companion
- **Target users:** College students who need to organize their subjects, study tasks, and quizzes.
- **Problem:** Students may have difficulty managing multiple study tasks and deadlines and preparing for quizzes. Our app brings these activities together in one place.

## Demo path

1. Open the app and select or create a subject. Add a study task with a deadline.
2. Open the Study Plan and mark the task as completed.
3. Take a quiz related to the selected subject, submit the answers, and view the score. Refresh the page to check whether the relevant data remains available.

## What works now

- Students can use the main subject and task workflow, including task deadlines and the Study Plan.
- The prototype demonstrates the subject-based quiz and result flow, with screenshots documenting the workflow and refresh-persistence check.

## What does not work yet

- The visual theme and color consistency need improvement, as identified during the Week 6 demo review.
- The end-to-end test results and exact tester details need to be confirmed after the theme fix and another test run.

**Last updated:**  2026-09-14

## 1. Project purpose

What problem are you solving?

College students often have difficulty organizing their study tasks, remembering assignment and exam deadlines, and preparing for exams. Smart Study Companion combines a simple study planner and quiz system to help students manage their study work and practice their knowledge in one place.

## 2. Target users

Who is this for?

- Primary user: College Students
- Secondary user, if any: None for the MVP

## 3. Smallest useful version

What is the smallest version that would still be useful?
The smallest useful version will allow students to:

Create and manage subjects
Add study tasks
Set assignment and exam deadlines
View upcoming tasks
Mark tasks as completed
Take simple multiple-choice quizzes
View quiz scores
### Rough user flow — 3–5 steps

Draw or describe the smallest user journey you can demonstrate. Put the user's action on each arrow and end with a visible result.

## 4. In scope

What are you building this semester?

- Subject and study task management
- Assignment and exam deadline management
- Multiple-choice quiz and score system
- Task completion tracking
- Simple upcoming-task view
## 5. Out of scope

What are you **not** building this semester?

- AI-based personalized study recommendations
- Complex notification or reminder systems
- Online multiplayer or real-time quiz competitions
- Advanced analytics and detailed performance reports
- Large-scale question database

## 6. Midterm demo sentence

Our midterm demo will show:

> A student creating a subject, adding study tasks and deadlines, marking a task as completed, taking a short multiple-choice quiz, and viewing the quiz score.

## 7. Final demo sentence

Our final demo will prove:

> A student can use Smart Study Companion to organize study tasks and deadlines, track completed work, take quizzes, and check quiz results in one simple application.

## 9. Risks and unknowns

| Risk / unknown | Why it matters | Plan |
|---|---|---|
|The application may become too complicated  |Too many features could make development difficult and reduce usability  |Focus on the core MVP features first  |
|Combining planner and quiz features may increase development time  |Both parts need to work together correctly  |Develop and test the planner and quiz features separately first  |
|Not having enough useful quiz questions  |Users need enough questions to practice effectively  |Start with a small set of questions and add more if time allows  |
|Deadline and task management may become confusing  |Students need to understand their upcoming work easily |Keep the interface simple and show only important task information  |
|Limited development time  |Extra features may prevent completion of the main system  |Treat reminders, timers, and advanced tracking as optional features  |

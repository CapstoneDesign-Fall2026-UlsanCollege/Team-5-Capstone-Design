## Selected direction

We chose: Smart Study Companion 

> Smart Study Companion as our primary project. It combines a Study Planner and College Quiz System to help college students organize their study tasks, manage deadlines, and prepare for exams. We believe the combined idea is useful while still being small enough to develop and demonstrate.

## Backup idea

Our backup idea is: PocketCart

> PocketCart, which combines a Grocery List and Smart Budget Planner. It will help college students and families organize grocery purchases, track expenses, and manage their monthly budget.

## Out of scope

We are **not** building:

- Advanced AI features, complex recommendation systems, or personalized study/budget predictions.
- Online payment, banking integration, online grocery ordering, or other external services.
- Advanced notifications, analytics, multiplayer features, or other features that are not necessary for the MVP.
